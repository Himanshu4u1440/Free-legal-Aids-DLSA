Porbandar Public Legal Aid Network

Who are the PLVs of DLSA Porbandar?

Under the authority of the Chairman, DLSA Porbandar (Principal District & Sessions Judge), this dedicated team of 44 certified Para Legal Volunteers serves as the grassroots bridge across Porbandar city, coastal belts, and rural talukas.

Whether a citizen encounters distress at a police station, an undertrial prisoner is detained in the Porbandar Special Sub-Jail, an injured patient arrives at Bhavsinhji Hospital, or an unorganised laborer needs legal counseling—our authorized PLVs are deployed to assist without charging any money.

Zero Charges for Common People: All PLV services are 100% Free of Cost. PLVs receive an official honorarium from the government and are strictly prohibited from demanding or receiving money, gifts, or loans.
Official Identity Card Rules

Each volunteer carries an official DLSA Porbandar Photo ID Card subject to 4 statutory conditions:

  1. Loss of ID card must be reported to the nearest police station immediately.
  2. Card shall NOT be used for bus or transport concessions.
  3. Card shall NOT be used for availing government loans or personal benefits.
  4. Card is strictly for public identification as an authorized DLSA Porbandar PLV.
Core Mandate

12 Statutory Duties of Porbandar PLVs

Prescribed under the NALSA & GSLSA Guidelines for Citizen Legal Empowerment

Grassroots Legal First-Aid

First point of contact for citizens in Porbandar, Chhaya, Bokhira, and rural settlements.

Police Station Remand Monitor

Ensuring detainees at City 'A' and 'B' Division Police Stations receive free legal representation upon detention.

Sub-Jail Porbandar Clinic Desk

Assisting indigent undertrials in preparing bail petitions and reviewing long detention under Section 436A.

Coastal Labor & Fishermen Rights

Guiding traditional fishermen and boat crew on safety entitlements, wage claims, and accident relief.

Women Distress & Sakhi Support

Assisting survivors of domestic violence to reach the Porbandar One Stop Centre and obtain protection orders.

Front Office Court Guidance

Operating the District Court Helpdesk, assisting illiterate litigants in filling free legal aid forms.

Lok Adalat Mobilization

Pre-litigation conciliation for compoundable disputes, traffic fines, and family settlements in Porbandar.

Senior Citizens Protection

Enforcing elderly parents' rights before the SDM Maintenance Tribunal against neglect or dispossession.

Bhavsinhji Hospital Trauma Desk

Assisting road accident and acid attack victims in filing for emergency relief under the Gujarat Compensation Scheme.

Child Welfare & POCSO Aid

Friendly representation before Child Welfare Committee (CWC) and Juvenile Justice Board Porbandar.

Village Legal Literacy Camps

Organizing awareness camps in Ranavav, Kutiyana, Jam Raval, and coastal settlements.

Reporting Violations to DLSA

Directly intimating the Secretary, DLSA Porbandar regarding illegal lockup detention or social exploitation.
